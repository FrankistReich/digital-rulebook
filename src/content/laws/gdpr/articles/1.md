## Abs. 1 {#p1}

:::en
This Regulation lays down rules relating to the protection of natural persons[^np] with regard to the processing of personal data and rules relating to the free movement of personal data.

[^np]: "Natural person" excludes legal persons (companies, associations). For corporate-data protection see Recital 14 and Article 4(1).
:::

:::zh
本条例规定了关于在个人数据处理方面保护自然人[^自然人]的规则，以及关于个人数据自由流动的规则。

[^自然人]: "自然人"不包括法人（公司、协会等）。关于法人数据保护，参见序言第 14 段及第 4 条第（1）项。
:::

## Abs. 2 {#p2}

:::en
This Regulation protects fundamental rights and freedoms of natural persons and in particular their right to the protection of personal data.
:::

:::zh
本条例保护自然人的基本权利和自由，尤其是其个人数据受保护的权利。
:::

## Abs. 3 {#p3}

:::en
The free movement of personal data within the Union shall be neither restricted nor prohibited for reasons connected with the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data.
:::

:::zh
不得出于与在个人数据处理方面保护自然人有关的理由，限制或禁止个人数据在欧盟内部的自由流动。
:::
